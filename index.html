On iPhone or iPad, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, for full screen.
  • A D or arrows: move
  • W or Space: jump
  • J sword (blocks arrows)
  • K shoot (auto-aims)
  • Shift roll (you can't be hit)
  • E use item, Q switch item
  • R switch ranged weapon
  • S drop through wood floors
  • P pause, M sound
  • Controller: plug in, press a button, then C on the title screen sets the buttons

Click the game once, then press Enter. Emeralds you win are saved on this device and buy upgrades on the title screen.
